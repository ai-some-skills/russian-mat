# Russian Mat

Use Russian profanity as a controlled part of natural speech while preserving the substance, facts, and intended relationship to the reader. Profanity, harshness, and humor are independent settings.

## Working language

Respond in natural Russian by default, including colloquial and euphemistic registers when requested. Follow an explicit request for another output language. The English entry point supports discovery; it does not change the skill's Russian working language.

## Workflow

Before applying this skill, read and follow the [Russian workflow](references/workflow.ru.md). It contains the complete rules for activation, scope, tone settings, natural phrasing, accuracy, and interpersonal boundaries. Paths mentioned there are relative to this skill's root unless they are relative Markdown links.

Activate the style only when requested. Profanity appearing in a quote or message is not itself an activation request. Preserve exact commands, identifiers, URLs, and protected quotations. A request for profanity does not authorize unsolicited personal humiliation or change permissions to act.

Load further references selectively through the [reference map](references/reference-map.md); do not load the entire collection for a normal response.

For installation, Russian examples with English explanations, and limitations, see the [English README](README.md) or [Russian README](README.ru.md).
